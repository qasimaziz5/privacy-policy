Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 September 2026. This policy explains what iCashPro processes, why it is needed, how sharing works and the choices available to you.

On this pageInformationHow it is usedStorage & syncAccount sharingDevice permissionsRetention & deletionSecurityChildrenPolicy changes

1. Information we process

Depending on the features you use, iCashPro may process:

  • Account identifiers such as user ID, display name, email address, authentication provider and Guest ID information.
  • Businesses, accounts, currencies, balances, transactions, notes, categories, payment modes, customer/contact details and movement history that you enter.
  • Images or other supported attachments, reminder settings and exported/backup data.
  • Sharing invitations, member roles, approval decisions, access permissions and the identity of the user who created or changed a shared record.
  • Device notification tokens, basic device/platform information and technical request data needed for sign-in, security, synchronization and delivery of notifications.

2. How information is used

Information is used to create and authenticate your account, provide cashbook features, calculate balances and reports, save and restore records, synchronize supported data, deliver email verification codes and reminders, enable account sharing, enforce roles, prevent abuse, diagnose failures and provide support.

Current access: All currently available app features are free. No subscription-based account limit is active. If optional paid plans are introduced later, pricing and limits will be shown before purchase.

3. Local storage, cloud backup and sync

iCashPro uses local device storage for responsive access and offline-capable work where supported. When you sign in or use sharing, selected records and backups may also be transmitted to and stored by contracted cloud service providers so data can synchronize, notifications can be delivered and supported recovery can occur. Internet access is required for authentication, email verification, invitations, shared updates, push notifications and cloud operations.

No system can guarantee uninterrupted availability or recovery of every record. Keep a separate JSON backup before signing out, replacing a device, deleting the app or restoring data.

User-to-user account sharing

An Owner can invite another registered iCashPro user using that user's registered email. The recipient must accept before access is granted. Owners must verify the recipient and share only information they are authorized to disclose.

Roles and approvals

  • Owner: controls account details, members, roles and deletion permission.
  • Admin: may manage members; a new invitation initiated by an Admin requires Owner approval before it is sent.
  • Editor: may add and edit transactions; deletion is available only when the Owner enables Allow Delete.
  • Viewer: has read-only access.

Shared activity and notifications

Authorized changes may synchronize to all members and may show who performed an action. Invitations, approvals, acceptance, rejection, role changes, removals and shared activity may generate in-app, email or push notifications. The Owner can remove a member and a member can leave. This stops future authorized access but cannot recall information already viewed, exported, copied or independently backed up.

4. Device permissions

  • Contacts: used only after permission to help select customer/contact information.
  • Photos/files: used when you select an attachment, import a backup or export a report.
  • Notifications: used for reminders and account-sharing activity when enabled.

You can change permissions in device settings, but disabling one may prevent the related feature from working.

5. Authentication and service providers

iCashPro may use Apple, Google, email verification and Guest ID sign-in. Contracted providers may process limited information to authenticate users, send verification email, host encrypted-in-transit cloud requests, store supported records and deliver notifications. Their own terms and privacy policies may also apply.

6. Disclosure and sale of data

We do not sell personal or financial data. Information may be disclosed to service providers only as needed to operate the app, to users you intentionally share an account with, when required by law, or to protect users, security and legal rights. iCashPro does not currently use your financial records for targeted advertising.

7. Retention, backup and deletion

Local records remain until you delete them, restore over them, delete the app or erase the device. Supported cloud records are kept while needed to provide the service, maintain sharing and recovery, prevent abuse or meet legal/security requirements. Deleting an account can permanently delete its transactions. Leaving a shared account removes your access, while the Owner's records remain.

To delete your iCashPro identity, first remove or leave all linked accounts, then use Settings → Delete My Account. You may also contact support. Some limited records may be retained where reasonably necessary for fraud prevention, security, legal compliance or resolving disputes.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures, including authenticated requests, role checks and encrypted network connections. You are responsible for securing your device, email account, verification codes, backups and sharing choices. No method of storage or transmission is completely risk-free.

9. International processing

Cloud providers and users of a shared account may be located in different countries. By using online features, information may be processed where those services operate, subject to applicable safeguards and law.

10. Children's privacy

iCashPro is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us so the matter can be reviewed.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when app features, security requirements, providers, law or the business model changes. The effective date will be updated and material changes may also be communicated in the app.
